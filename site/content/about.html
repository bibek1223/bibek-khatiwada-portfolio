The short version

From SEO intern to strategist, one system at a time.

I’m an SEO strategist at RankMeTop, working remotely from Kathmandu, and I also take on independent consulting. Over 3+ years I have worked across 150+ projects, 100+ niches, 10+ live sites, and clients in six countries.

My work sits where strategy meets implementation: entity-based SEO and topical authority, technical audits, on-page and off-page execution, content systems, reporting, and AI search optimization. When a tool I need does not exist, I build it.

I look for relationships—between entities, pages, queries, systems, and business outcomes—rather than working through isolated checklist items. That habit comes straight from how I was trained.

Content Semantic Flow Checker

An open-source Python app that checks whether content flows logically. It embeds each sentence, paragraph, and section (H1, H2, H3) with a sentence-transformers model and measures cosine similarity between neighbours, flagging abrupt topic jumps against tuned thresholds.
