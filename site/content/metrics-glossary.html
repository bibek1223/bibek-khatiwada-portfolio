Reading the numbers

What each metric means.

All figures come from Google Search Console’s Performance report for organic web search.

Clicks

How many times a searcher clicked through to the site from Google Search results. This is the closest Search Console gets to “visits from Google”, but it is not the same as sessions in analytics.

Impressions

How many times a link to the site appeared in results a searcher saw. Impressions measure visibility, not traffic—a site can have huge impressions and few clicks.

Average CTR

Click-through rate: clicks divided by impressions. It depends heavily on position, the type of query, and how much of the answer Google shows directly in the results.

Average position

The average ranking of the site’s highest result across all impressions, where 1 is the top of page one. Large sites ranking for many long-tail queries usually have a higher (worse-looking) average than small, focused sites.

Why a 16-month window?

Sixteen months is the longest period Search Console shows in its Performance report, so it captures the fullest history available for each project.
