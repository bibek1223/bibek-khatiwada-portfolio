The short version

This is a static website. It has no user accounts, no database, no cookies of its own, and no analytics or advertising trackers. It cannot store anything you type into it.

The brief builder on the contact page

The brief builder runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to this website. When you press a send button, the text is handed to your own email app or to WhatsApp, and you decide whether to send it from there.

When you contact me

Messages you send by email, WhatsApp, phone, or LinkedIn reach me through those services and are used only to reply to you and discuss your project. To ask me to delete our correspondence, email {{email}}.

Third parties

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google’s servers, which receive your IP address. See Google’s privacy policy. Like most websites, the hosting provider may keep standard server logs (IP address, time, and page requested) for security and operations.
