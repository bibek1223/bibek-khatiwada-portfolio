The operating model

A finding is not finished until the live result is verified.

The work moves through a visible chain: evidence, decision, implementation, release, measurement. That keeps strategy connected to production and gives every recommendation an owner, an expected outcome, and a way to check whether it worked.

The rule

Finding → plan → implementation → live result → verification.

A ticket marked “done” is not proof that search engines received the intended change.

        Four stages

        The delivery cycle stays simple enough to follow.

        The artifacts change with the problem. The decision logic does not.

        01

        Diagnose

        Reconstruct what changed using Search Console, analytics, crawl data, release history, templates, and business context. Separate symptoms from causes.

        Typical outputs
        • Evidence ledger
        • Issue map by template or directory
        • Prioritized findings with confidence
        02

        Architect

        Translate findings into page roles, entity relationships, technical rules, ownership, sequencing, and measurable acceptance criteria.

        Typical outputs
        • Page and query map
        • Technical specification
        • 90-day priority plan
        03

        Implement

        Ship changes through the real CMS, templates, content workflow, redirects, schema, internal links, automation, and team handoffs.

        Typical outputs
        • Production-ready briefs
        • QA checklist and change log
        • Automated monitors or dashboards
        04

        Verify

        Confirm the change is live, crawlable, rendered as intended, and moving the agreed signal. Record what worked and what needs another iteration.

        Typical outputs
        • Release verification
        • GSC and analytics annotations
        • Outcome review and next queue
