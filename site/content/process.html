How an engagement runs

From evidence to verified growth, in four stages.

Clear enough for a team to follow, rigorous enough to support real decisions. Select a stage.

Find what is actually limiting growth.

I review Search Console, analytics, site structure, crawl data, and technical signals before recommending anything. After an algorithm drop, that means tracing losses to specific updates.

  • Search Console and GA4 analysis
  • Technical and crawl audit
  • Competitive and entity gap analysis
  • Prioritized action plan

Give the site a coherent shape.

I map the topic, entity relationships, page types, and internal-link structure so every page has a job and pages stop competing with each other.

  • Topical map and clusters
  • Page-type and template plan
  • Internal-linking model
  • Consolidation and pruning rules

Ship the changes where they count.

I apply the changes across templates, content, schema, crawl rules, and reporting systems, and automate the repetitive parts so delivery scales.

  • Template and on-page changes
  • Content briefs and writing
  • Schema, canonicals, redirects, robots
  • Automation for repeat work

Track gains, catch regressions, keep tuning.

I track results, watch for regressions, and keep adjusting as search behaviour and algorithms shift, with reporting that explains what changed and why.

  • Looker Studio dashboards
  • KPI and rank tracking
  • AI-answer visibility tracking
  • Weekly or monthly reporting
